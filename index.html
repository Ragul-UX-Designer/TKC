Overview

A French-themed address worthy of its name

The King's Court is a thoughtfully master-planned, gated community of premium villa plots in the fast-growing neighbourhood of Kundrathur, South-West Chennai. Inspired by timeless French architecture, the enclave brings together grand entrance gateways, cobblestone avenues, landscaped gardens and a feature-rich clubhouse — a setting designed for graceful, secure and elevated everyday living.

CMDA & RERA approved and ready for your dream home, every plot sits within a fortified, fully-amenitised layout with round-the-clock security. Choose your plot, build your French-inspired villa, and enjoy a community crafted for both comfortable living and long-term investment value.
